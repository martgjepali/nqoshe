## Direction contract

THESIS: Fall i kafes, the Albanian reading of coffee grounds in the bottom of a cup. Nëqoshe reads the city's corners the way someone reads your cup: turn it over, let the sediment settle, and be told what it says. The page refuses the category default, a filterable grid of café cards above a map, and refuses its predictable opposite, a moody photo essay with no data in it. Both facts and feeling ride in the same object: the cup interior IS the corner, the sediment IS the map, the reading IS the recommendation.

OWN-WORLD: One global clock owns the page; scroll position is an hour of the Tirana day and every section reads from it. Grounds are fields, not accents: cream `#FAF3E7` at dawn, midnight roast `#1A120C` through the middle acts, warm dusk at the close, each act one saturated field rather than a tinted section. Terracotta `#C1512F` is the ink that marks a reading; brass `#D89A4A` is emitted light, never a fill; olive `#5C6B42` is the living thing on the table. Material: worn wood, glazed ceramic, sediment grain, and light that has a direction and a source. Type sets at poster scale with real nerve. Components are cup-and-saucer objects, sediment plots, and annotated light bands, never same-size cards with an icon on top.

STORY: A local and a visitor arrive at the same moment of need: where do I sit for the next three hours. They understand within one viewport that this reads corners rather than listing cafés, believe it because the readings carry facts nobody else publishes (noise, measured wifi, outlets, a work score) and are honest about what they do not know, and act by finding their corner.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cream field at low morning sun. The wordmark Nëqoshe set at poster scale, left-anchored on a wide asymmetric measure, revealing letter by letter as if light were crossing it. Albanian tagline leads, English supports beneath at a fraction of the scale. Right of the fold, a single ceramic cup catches raking golden-hour light with steam drifting; it responds to cursor within the first second. A thin brass rule carries the hour of the Tirana day and moves as the visitor scrolls. Primary action sits on the light, left, below the tagline: Gjej këndin tënd.

FORM: Fall i kafes, candidate 5 of 7 on the grounded list, assigned by the roll. Seed key 61fd531e. Raised by three named donations: SINGLE CLOCK, from the algorave challenger, one page-wide time authority instead of scattered per-section motion. TIMESTAMPED LIGHT, from the daylight-section challenger, real hours off the dataset annotated against a traveling light band. ONE FIELD PER ACT, from the Saul Bass challenger, each act commits to its own full-bleed ground rather than tinting a shared one.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
